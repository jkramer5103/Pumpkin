# Sitting dragon scanning

Pumpkin had no handler for vanilla `DragonPhase:6`. A dragon restored in that phase normally kept hovering, while landing and breath completion immediately started another roar. The missing scan prevented the choice between watching a nearby player, roaring after 25 scanning ticks, and leaving the perch after 100 ticks without a close target.

The new handler restores phase 6, turns toward the nearest visible attackable player within 20 blocks and 10 vertical blocks, and starts the roar on tick 26. Without a close target, it takes off on tick 100; a visible attackable player within 150 blocks selects the existing charging handler. Landing and breath completion now enter scanning. Entry resets the timer and clears Pumpkin's shared flight target. The existing dragon NoAI gate pauses the timer.

Reference: Minecraft 26.3 `DragonSittingScanningPhase.doServerTick/begin`, with entry points in `DragonLandingPhase.doServerTick` and `DragonSittingFlamingPhase.doServerTick`. Decompiled Java remains outside Git. Fork master was already an ancestor. Current upstream and open PRs 3407/3661 do not implement scanning; their hitbox and crystal fixes were not duplicated.

Validation:

- All 472 `pumpkin-core` library tests, workspace formatting, core Clippy with all targets/features, and the debug server build passed under the shared Cargo lock and resource limits.
- The saved-phase regression fails when the old unsupported phase-6 decode gate is restored. A focused rotation regression covers the aligned-heading dead zone and head-relative turn distance.
- `dragon-sitting-scanning.json` compares phase restoration, the roar/takeoff deadlines, creative/spectator exclusion, horizontal/vertical bounds, distant charge selection, and a NoAI pause. Across two trials, vanilla and patched Pumpkin each pass 69/69 checks; baseline passes 29/69. Stimuli and fixture/binary hashes match, the headless player remains present, and all three servers stop without logged errors or panics. `dragon-scanning-entry.json` covers entry after a full breath and at a controlled podium top; across two trials, vanilla and patched Pumpkin each pass 27/27 checks, while baseline passes 19/27 with eight missing phase-6 observations. All dragons remain persistent and the observer remains connected. The same stimulus, fixture/binary hashes and stopped/error-free server checks pass. Combined results are vanilla/patched 96/96 and baseline 48/96, over 1,450 controlled ticks per target across the two fixtures.

The first exploratory vanilla run lost its stationary headless player to the anti-flight check; affected samples are invalid. Later reference attempts were interrupted before producing results. Final isolated references allow flight for that client. An initial entry fixture omitted persistence and baseline lost its second breath dragon while the observer was farther than 32 blocks; the existing generic despawn path can randomly remove it there. Those affected baseline samples are not counted as scanning evidence. The final entry fixture makes every dragon persistent and checks that property. These checks provide server-state evidence, without rendered-client or complete boss-fight proof. Vanilla final-save completion is unverified within the harness shutdown grace; all processes stop, and Pumpkin logs completed saves.

Limits: the shared targeting predicate still lacks vanilla sneak/invisibility distance scaling. The existing charge handler follows a player instead of a fixed point and has different completion logic. Exact turning trajectories, the four-flame counter, landing movement, multipart contacts, and the broader fight lifecycle remain separate work.
