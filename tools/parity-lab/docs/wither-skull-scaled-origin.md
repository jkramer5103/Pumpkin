# Wither skull launch offsets with scale

Source fix: `ff9b8cb3fb165de2aa42b5524ed095099ba2af21`. Validated source and regression fix.

Vanilla 26.3 `WitherBoss.getHeadX/Y/Z` multiplies head offsets by `LivingEntity.getScale`, which reads the effective SCALE attribute as a float. Pumpkin used fixed offsets. The constructor anchoring defect was already fixed by 5383388; this task covers nondefault scale separately. Only the Wither head helpers change; the skull constructor, spawn path, targeting and plugin events retain their existing behavior.

The bounded test resets position and motion before each single tick, with a connected creative headless client, a passive target 19 blocks away, easy difficulty and mob griefing disabled. Captured spawn packets show vanilla half-scale main/side heights 1.5/1.1 and side radius 0.65; double-scale heights 6/4.4 and radius 2.6. Baseline Pumpkin emits heights 3/2.2 and radius 1.3 at both scales. Skulls are removed immediately after observation to isolate launch geometry from collision and damage.

Permanent fixtures `wither-skull-scaled-half.json` and `wither-skull-scaled-double.json` require a naturally fired side-head skull on the scaled launch ring and retain boss/target health controls. Each uses 20 single-tick observation windows; packet probes use 45 to include a main-head attack. Two trials per scale use identical gameplay command hashes across targets. Vanilla and patched Pumpkin each pass 80/80 focused checks (46 packet, 34 fixture); baseline passes 64/80, failing exactly 12 packet origins and four fixture launch rings. Patched comparisons retain target health and connected-player controls, and match source/binary/fixture hashes. The initial vanilla probe's raw `main_seen` flag used a default-scale height; the evaluator independently identifies main shots by their later tick window and checks every captured position at the requested scale. Raw packets and that erroneous diagnostic flag are preserved.

Upstream master 998c3f8 retains unscaled head offsets. Open PRs 4092, 3835, 3845 and 3793 do not implement this correction. Fork master 65bca3f is already an ancestor; no additional existing campaign changes require integration.
