# Wither powered trident immunity

Source commit: `3607b36ba93f737d75ce1d86c30f54a1651a17c8`.

A powered Wither previously took damage from real thrown tridents. Vanilla's `WitherBoss.hurtServer` rejects direct `AbstractArrow` sources at or below half health, and `ThrownTrident` extends `AbstractArrow`. Pumpkin's corresponding explicit entity list omitted tridents. In addition, its trident collision called `damage` without supplying the projectile or attacker, preventing source-based immunity checks.

The Wither now includes tridents in that direct-source check. Trident impacts supply the projectile and owner (or projectile when ownerless) in their respective damage-context slots. This prerequisite reuses the minimal trident attribution change from [upstream PR 3835](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3835), head `3640e87c42efc5d582ae10d797c891a327067760`; the PR has no Wither immunity correction. Its broader flight, collision and projectile-state changes are outside this patch. Vanilla `ThrownTrident.onHitEntity` likewise constructs a trident damage source using the projectile and its owner, with the projectile as the ownerless fallback.

Seven focused regression fixtures cover real trident impacts at 149, 150 and 151 health, a trident/sheep damage context, Wither/friend attacker immunity, and a nonprojectile direct source carrying the trident damage type. The last control requires damage acceptance: this change checks projectile identity, rather than blocking the damage type indiscriminately. All use isolated frozen arenas and NoAI bosses to prevent regeneration or targeting from obscuring health changes.

All 443 core Rust library tests, workspace formatting, core Clippy with all targets/features and the full Pumpkin server build passed under the shared build lock and required resource limits. The existing `proc-macro-error2` future-compatibility warning remains. Two repetitions of seven fixtures gave vanilla and patched Pumpkin 82/82 passing checks each, and baseline Pumpkin 76/82 with exactly the six expected powered-trident health failures. Real impacts at 149 and 150 health leave vanilla and patched Withers unchanged; baseline loses 7.744 health. At 151 health, all three accept the hit and reach 143.256 health. The nonprojectile trident-damage control reaches 146.32 health on all three.

Matching fixture/gameplay-command hashes, eight verified impact ticks per real-trident case, cleanup barriers, connected headless protocol 777 clients, source/binary hashes and three normal scratch-server stops were verified. Server logs contain no ERROR or panic lines. Final binary SHA-256: `e7efbfecf0f70df2a1c55beda9b36e3caab0da2247a1037711149a458ad003f1`. This is headless server-state evidence; no rendered-client capture or complete boss-fight proof is claimed.

Final comparison validation is in `artifacts/jaron-wither23-validation.json`. Rust results are in `artifacts/jaron-wither23-verify.json`; arena reports are in `artifacts/jaron-wither23-{baseline,vanilla,fixed}-*/report.json`. Shared impact: the trident attribution call reaches existing entity damage hooks, plugin entity-damage events, source/cause packets, shielding/knockback and attacker-credit paths. It retains the existing hit sound, bounce and damage amount. Real owned throws, those shared side effects, enchantments, player collisions, projectile lifetime and full boss fights are outside the focused validation. Explicit command contexts isolate Wither attacker handling; they do not prove an owned throw. The current Breeze wind-charge entry and skull explosion attribution remain separate leads.

Fork master `65bca3f` remains an ancestor. Final upstream review fetched `998c3f8`; the commits since the tested `b57d821` baseline do not change the Wither, trident, mob or living damage paths. This patch does not integrate unrelated dependency, world-format or block-data changes. Open PRs were checked again before push; the attribution prerequisite above is the only reused overlap.
