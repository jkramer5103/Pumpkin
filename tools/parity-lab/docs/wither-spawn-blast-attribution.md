# Wither spawn explosion attribution

Source commit: `fd6117bc15719ff1e0375c176844ab1319aec19b`.

When a Wither finishes its spawn invulnerability, nearby Withers should reject its explosion damage. Pumpkin previously created an anonymous explosion, losing the boss identity required by its existing damage-immunity hook. A one-tick isolated arena reproduced full-health observers falling from 300 to 280.31183 HP and powered observers from 150 to 130.31184 HP; vanilla 26.3 retained both initial health values.

The spawn-completion call now uses `World::explode_with_source` with the spawning Wither. The existing explosion code resolves that living source as its own cause, selects the attributed explosion damage type and excludes the source from the blast query. It supplies the boss identity to the existing damage hooks. Radius, position, countdown, regeneration, block interaction, plugin-event dispatch, packets and sound still use the same helpers. This is a separate caller from the previously fixed skull-impact explosion.

Vanilla review followed `WitherBoss.customServerAiStep`, `ServerLevel.explode`, `ServerExplosion`'s damage-source construction and entity query, `Explosion.getIndirectSourceEntity`, `DamageSources.explosion`, and `WitherBoss.hurtServer`. Vanilla passes the boss itself to the explosion; its living indirect source is the same boss, and Withers reject damage caused by a Wither. No vanilla source is published here.

The opt-in regression fixture `lab/examples/wither-spawn-blast-immunity.json` completes `Invul:1` beside full-health and powered NoAI Withers and a sheep. Initial health, the final countdown, source health, sheep damage and absence of skulls prevent a missing explosion from appearing as a pass. Supplemental cases separately exercise the two observer health states, sheep damage, the preceding countdown tick, NoAI suppression and ordinary TNT damage. Observers have NoAI to exclude regeneration; all arenas are isolated and use a connected creative headless client.

All 443 core Rust library tests, workspace formatting, core Clippy with all targets/features, and the full debug server build passed under the shared Cargo lock with one job, disabled debug info/incremental compilation, and the required resource caps. The existing `proc-macro-error2` future-compatibility warning remains. Two trials of six isolated cases plus two permanent-fixture trials gave vanilla and patched Pumpkin **126/126** passing checks each (102 supplemental, 24 fixture). Baseline passed **118/126**, failing exactly eight expected Wither-immunity assertions. Full-health and powered observers retain 300 and 150 HP after the real spawn explosion. The sheep is killed; TNT still damages Withers. The preceding countdown tick and NoAI prevent premature explosions. TNT controls check damage acceptance, not exact damage amounts.

Commands and fixture hashes match across all three targets. Source and binary hashes were verified; the patched binary SHA-256 is `763636e85fe1c33a22392aabae3cf2c93bebc9fee9de6c5552b51bc9d2854565`. Evidence: `artifacts/jaron-wither27-validation.json`, `artifacts/jaron-wither27-verify.json`, `artifacts/jaron-wither27-source-provenance.json` and `artifacts/jaron-wither27-log-review.json`. The scratch clients stayed connected; server/client logs contain no errors or panics. All six scratch processes stopped, with complete shutdown markers verified for the four Pumpkin runs. Limitations: headless server-state evidence only, no rendered-client capture or complete boss fight. Generic explosion damage amounts, knockback, credit, plugin cancellation and block destruction are not independently validated by this patch. Vanilla complete-shutdown markers remain unverified. The separate head-angle/body-rotation lead remains unconfirmed and intersects the AI-foundation work. Exact skull-head slot correspondence is also a source lead requiring packet-based reproduction before any edit.
