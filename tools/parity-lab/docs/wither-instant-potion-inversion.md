# Wither instant-potion inversion

Withers must take damage from direct instant-healing potions and heal from instant-harming potions. Pumpkin's direct potion dispatcher used the ordinary heal/harm direction for every entity. The correction consults the generated `minecraft:inverted_healing_and_harm` entity tag and rounds scaled instant potency to whole health points. The existing Wither arrow-effect rejection remains before this dispatch.

The official 26.3 `HealOrHarmMobEffect.applyInstantaneousEffect` selects healing when `isHarm == mob.isInvertedHealAndHarm()`, then truncates scaled potency plus 0.5. `LivingEntity.isInvertedHealAndHarm` reads the entity tag. `ThrownSplashPotion.onHitAsPotion` calls that method directly, bypassing the Wither's `addEffect` rejection. The source is the official jar, decompiled outside Git. This ports the dispatch and rounding, keeping the existing coefficients, damage/event path and application-source scale handling.

The shared callers are splash impacts, area-effect clouds, drinking/consumption, witches and tipped arrows. Entities outside the inversion tag retain their heal/harm direction and gain whole-point potency rounding. Tagged entities invert direction. Wither arrows still return before any application. Existing damage attribution, boss damage gates, geometric splash falloff, duration scaling and effect tick scheduling are separate concerns.

Exploratory real item throws in isolated arenas confirmed the difference: a Wither at 200 health ends at 194 after healing I and 204 after harming I on official 26.3. Pumpkin baseline ends at 203.9 and 194.15. Potions are moved immediately after a real protocol item-use packet to break against bedrock at the stationary target's feet, isolating the direct effect. An earlier setup left vanilla's projectile flying inside the boss and is excluded; it was not evidence of immunity. Frozen-tick tracking can remove a Pumpkin projectile before its spawn packet is sent; the regression instead establishes the real throw with an item-use packet, exactly one queried projectile before impact, its NBT, and impact events/removal.
