# Dragon flight movement and drag

With a hover target established, place a dragon 30 blocks along positive Z from its target, facing it, and give it Motion [1,1,0]. Vanilla moves one block in X and Y on the next tick. Baseline Pumpkin moves only 0.914713 blocks in X and 0.91 blocks in Y, then applies ordinary living drag again. A perched targetless dragon also moves on baseline Pumpkin, while vanilla preserves its position and motion.

The correction moves dragons inside steer_toward, after acceleration and before flight damping. Dragons bypass ordinary living travel and its small-velocity cutoff; other living entities retain their path. Targetless and NoAI dragons have no flight movement. Dying-phase flight uses the same steering method; corpse ascent remains separate. The existing in_wall flag selects vanilla's 0.8 displacement factor without scaling stored velocity, and vertical damping uses vanilla's float factor converted to double. Detection of in_wall remains separate work.

Reference: official 26.3 EnderDragon.aiStep and DragonHoverPhase. Current upstream master c7a234ab8 retains the flight defect. Fork master was already an ancestor of the campaign branch. Fresh reviews found no equivalent dragon correction in [PR3407](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3407), [PR3661](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3661), [PR3835](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3835) or [PR4092](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/4092). PR4092 changes shared living AI/travel, so future integration must preserve the dragon exclusion. Multipart and projectile work remain deferred to the existing PRs.

Vanilla and patched Pumpkin passed 86/86 checks; baseline passed 54/86, failing exactly 10 position-distance assertions and 22 position/motion/rotation vector comparisons. Controls cover straight and inertial flight, three-tick momentum, small velocity, targetless perched behavior and NoAI.
- The retained dragon-flight-movement.json world regression supplies positive position-distance assertions. Private comparison additionally checks recorded Pos, Motion and Rotation against vanilla within 0.0001. Fixture, stimulus, Rust source and copied-binary hashes match; the baseline binary is traced to the prior campaign source commit. Connected headless clients and stopped scratch servers were verified. No ERROR/panic lines were observed; both Pumpkin saves completed.

Limits: headless protocol-client and server-state checks only. No rendered-client capture, complete fight, natural circling trajectory, dying-flight or in-wall slowdown proof. This world-dependent regression has no new isolated Rust unit test. Vanilla final-save completion was not observed within the shutdown grace period, though process stop was verified.
