# Regenerated dragon respawn crystal protection

During dragon respawn, regenerated pillar crystals must resist ordinary damage until the ritual completes or is aborted. Pumpkin's respawn-only `DragonFight::regenerate_spike` explicitly made them vulnerable. A player could consequently destroy a newly regenerated crystal during the summoning animation. The fix initializes that crystal as invulnerable. Ordinary initial-fight crystals remain vulnerable, and the existing completion/abort paths release regenerated crystals through `reset_spike_crystals`.

Official 26.3 `DragonRespawnStage.SUMMONING_PILLARS.tick` constructs `EndSpikeFeature` with `crystalInvulnerable = true`. `EndSpikeFeature.placeSpike` passes that setting to `EndCrystal.setPermanentlyInvulnerable` before spawning the crystal. `EndCrystal.hurtServer` rejects ordinary damage through the base invulnerability gate. Destroying a portal crystal during respawn invokes `EnderDragonFight.onCrystalDestroyed`, which resets pillar crystals. The same reset runs when respawn completes. The Rust regeneration helper has one caller, in the pillar summoning stage; normal crystal spawning uses a separate helper.

The focused fixture starts a fresh isolated End fight, checks for its initial dragon every tick during bounded setup, kills that dragon, discovers the real exit podium, and starts respawn with three summoned crystals and a fourth placed by an actual protocol 777 item-use packet. After exactly 185 observation ticks, two pillars have regenerated. It damages the first crystal with one point of generic damage, verifies an ordinary crystal can be destroyed with the same damage command, then breaks a portal crystal and verifies the second pillar crystal becomes destructible. The second crystal's UUID is checked across the abort so a replacement cannot satisfy that control. Four initial center beams, pillar positions, exact stepped game-time interval, client presence, cleanup and process shutdown are required.

Before the patch, official vanilla rejects damage and preserves the regenerated crystal; the Pumpkin campaign 70 binary accepts damage and removes it. Both targets pass ordinary-crystal and abort-reset controls. Source hashes prove the baseline's dragon fight file is identical to the patch preimage. The focused checker deliberately requires this baseline failure and requires protection on vanilla and patched Pumpkin. It accepts the existing `1` versus `1.0` command feedback formatting difference.

Limits: headless protocol and server-state evidence only; no rendered client, Bedrock, natural player attack, creative/bypass damage, save/reload, full pillar geometry, successful ritual completion or whole boss fight proof. Pillar heights vary with generation, so the fixture checks horizontal columns and uses actual server positions. Regenerated crystal center beams remain a separate unvalidated lead; this patch changes only invulnerability.
