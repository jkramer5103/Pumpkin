# Wither skull blast damage behind a solid wall

A Wither skull hitting a wall previously left a fully covered nearby mob unharmed. Vanilla applies the explosion calculator even when exposure is zero, producing 1 HP of damage within the blast radius. Pumpkin returned before that call. A cow at 10 HP therefore remained at 10 instead of falling to 9; a cow at 1 HP survived instead of dying.

The shared explosion loop now skips zero-exposure knockback only after applying damage. Radius checks, damage eligibility, source attribution, boss immunity and plugin events still use the existing path. Calculators that return false from `should_damage_entity` still skip damage. Other default-calculator explosions also receive this minimum damage, as in vanilla; their individual gameplay callers have not yet been exercised for this patch. Open-air damage scaling is a separate existing difference and is outside this correction.

Vanilla 26.3 source review followed `WitherSkull.onHit`, `ServerExplosion.hurtEntities` and `ExplosionDamageCalculator.getEntityDamageAmount`. The damage call is independent of exposure; only its multiplier and knockback become zero. This patch retains Pumpkin's optimization of skipping a zero velocity update. Fetched fork master is already an ancestor of the source branch. Fetched upstream `998c3f8` still contains the early return. Fresh file reviews of [PR 3835](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3835) and [PR 3905](https://github.com/Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin/pull/3905) cover projectile flight/direct impacts and launch/effects/exposure raycasts respectively; neither moves this damage gate. No upstream code was copied.

Two isolated headless-client trials capture naturally fired Wither skulls, pause their shooters, then drive each skull into an intact stone wall. Covered surviving and lethal cows, invulnerable cows, outside-radius cows and shooter health are checked. Vanilla and patched Pumpkin each passed 52/52 meaningful checks; baseline Pumpkin passed 44/52, failing exactly the eight covered-damage/survival assertions. Four inherited vacuous cleanup assertions are excluded; all reference scratch processes stopped. The permanent opt-in fixture `lab/examples/wither-skull-occluded-blast.json` separately covers ordinary and dangerous skulls, exact health, wall retention, protected/distant controls and zero victim velocity. Its two final trials gave vanilla and patched Pumpkin 46/46 each and baseline 42/46, failing exactly four minimum-damage assertions. Explicit death-removal barriers and absence checks prevent a dying prior cow from satisfying the next case. Earlier fixture trials without that isolation are excluded.
