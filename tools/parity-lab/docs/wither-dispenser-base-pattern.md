# Dispenser construction of Withers

Pumpkin required a complete three-skull pattern, counting the prospective target skull, before a dispenser could place a Wither skull. An empty skull row therefore remained empty, preventing automatic construction. Vanilla 26.3 permits placement on the soul-block base before any skulls are present. Its eligibility check also rejects Peaceful placement without consuming the skull item.

Vanilla reference: DispenseItemBehavior$7.execute checks target air and WitherSkullBlock.canSpawnMob. That method checks the build-height lower boundary and non-Peaceful difficulty, then matches a base pattern whose top row has no predicates. After placement, checkSpawn uses the complete pattern and initializes the Wither. Pumpkin now shares its existing soul-block/air-corner matcher between the base and complete patterns, retaining full skull matching for actual summoning. The dispenser's equipment fallback and effects retain their previous ordering.

The wither-dispenser-base-pattern.json regression scenario powers three north-facing dispensers one at a time in three skull-placement orders. It checks each intermediate skull, dispenser item consumption, all seven consumed structure blocks, the final boss and spawn-invulnerability state. Controls cover a precompleted skull pair, Peaceful, a missing soul-block arm, an occupied target and ordinary skeleton skulls beside the target. Each target runs two trials with a connected headless observer and verified simulation ticks in isolated scratch worlds.

Validation on 2026-10-20: vanilla and patched Pumpkin each passed 284/284 checks, while the prepatch campaign binary passed 204/284. The 80 baseline failures are exactly the unfinished skull-row cases, Peaceful item/block preservation, and placement next to ordinary skulls. The precompleted skull-pair, missing-arm and occupied-target controls passed on both versions. All 16 rows and 593 controlled ticks completed per target.
